Insulation that does not look like insulation.

False lintels

A shaped lintel above each opening restores the proportions the render would otherwise flatten.

False corner stones

Quoins formed on the insulation give the corners of the house their weight back.

Decorative stone

Stone finish applied directly on the insulation, in panels or full elevations.

Thermal bridges are treated, not skipped. Reveals, sills, eaves and corners get the same attention as the open wall, and all work respects the standards that apply.

EXTERNAL WALL INSULATION / DUBLIN

Stop heating the street.

Heat leaves a house through walls, corners, sills and eaves. We wrap the whole outside in rock wool or EPS polystyrene, from the starting rail to the finish, and treat every thermal bridge on the way.

Four places a wrap usually fails.

01Corners. Two walls meet, the insulation thins. We reinforce and finish them, or form false corner stones over them. 02Window reveals and lintels. The wall gets deeper, so the opening needs wrapping, not stopping at the frame. 03Sills. New aluminium sills are fitted so water drains clear of the thicker wall. 04Eaves and gutters. Gutters and accessories come off and go back on after the wrap.
